# Student Questionnaire

We are studying how students use the bASICs FPGA and ASIC learning environment.
Your feedback can help us find setup problems, unclear instructions, and useful next features.

The questionnaire takes approximately 5–8 minutes.
Participation is voluntary, and you can stop at any time.
Your decision will not affect your grade, program status, or access to bASICs.

The form does not request your name, email address, school, or student ID.
The server records your IP address for duplicate detection and abuse review.
Do not include other personal information in a written answer.

The research team will not include IP addresses in the report.
It will report grouped results and short de-identified comments.
The approved questionnaire contains more information about consent, data storage, and data retention.
